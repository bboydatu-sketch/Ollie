Ollie AAC PWA v1.5 — Playful, calm, and multilingual

Included: English, Bahasa Melayu, and Tagalog; all 18 illustrated default cards; existing card and photo storage; singing girl app icon; offline cache; caregiver settings.

Deploy to GitHub Pages:
1. Download and unzip Ollie_PWA_v1_5_Playful.zip.
2. In your existing Ollie GitHub repository, upload and replace all seven files at the repository root: index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, practice.js, and README.txt.
3. Commit the changes. GitHub Pages will publish the update.
4. On iPhone, open the Ollie website in Safari and refresh. If the old version remains, close and reopen Safari or remove and re-add the Home Screen shortcut.

Use Settings to select language, choose 2 or 4 large cards per page, turn on low-stimulation mode, or try gentle cause-and-effect bubbles. Card taps speak the label using the device speech voice and give a brief visual response. Sound starts from the tap, which supports iPhone Safari audio rules. Filipino voice availability depends on iOS installed voices.

Your customized vocabulary and photos are stored on the device and are not overwritten by replacing the website files. Keep a backup of the old ZIP before deploying.

Communication practice module: choose turn-taking, two-choice communication, sound play, or song pauses. Activities are child-led, brief, and accept gestures, looks, AAC, sounds, and words as communication. The module is available in English, Malay, and Tagalog. It does not track scores or require spoken responses.
